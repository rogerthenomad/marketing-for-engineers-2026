# Credits

## The original

This project exists because of **[Marketing for Engineers](https://github.com/goabstract/Marketing-for-Engineers)**,
created and maintained by **Lisa Dziuba** and **Ahmed Sulaiman**, founders of Flawless App.

Between roughly 2017 and 2018 they hand-curated one of the best marketing reading lists
ever written for engineers. It taught a generation of indie developers that marketing is a
learnable skill and not a dark art. If you have ever shipped a side project and figured out
how to get its first hundred users, you owe those two something.

The original repository carries no `LICENSE` file. For that reason **this edition does not
copy its text.** Every chapter here was written from scratch in 2026. What we inherited is
the *idea*: a practical, opinionated, engineer-facing marketing collection, organised by the
problem you are actually trying to solve.

Where a resource from the original list is still genuinely the best thing available in 2026,
it is re-recommended here on its own merits, linked directly to its author.

## What happened to the original

- Last substantive content updates: circa 2018.
- The repository moved from `LisaDziuba/Marketing-for-Engineers` to
  `goabstract/Marketing-for-Engineers`. Many in-page anchor links in the original still point
  at the old `LisaDziuba` URLs.
- Flawless App, the tool it was written to market, is no longer operating.
- A large share of the linked tools and articles are now dead, paywalled, or describe
  platforms that no longer behave the way the article assumes.

None of that is a criticism. It is simply what an eight-year-old link collection looks like,
and it is the reason this edition exists.
